Overview

XUsPomodoro is a lightweight focus timer that helps you work in structured blocks. No account, no streaks, and nothing competing for the attention it is meant to protect.

Highlights

  • One timer, two breaks. Breaks never start on their own.
  • Three quick timers. For everything that is not a Pomodoro.
  • Session history. Label sessions and see weekly stats in your history.
  • Accurate. It counts to an end time, so a sleeping laptop cannot stretch a session.
  • Works offline. It runs in your browser, nothing to download, and everything stays there.
  • Left out on purpose. No auto-start, streaks, daily goals, or badges.
