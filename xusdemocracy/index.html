Overview

XUsDemocracy shows who represents you and when you vote next, from one address. It replaces a handful of government websites and keeps nothing you type.

Highlights

  • Your representatives. Officials at every level, with real phone numbers and contact links.
  • The national picture. From the President and Cabinet to the Supreme Court.
  • Your next election. Primary and general dates, plus your state’s registration deadline.
  • Election calendar. Subscribe once and election dates appear in your calendar app.
  • Nonpartisan by design. It shows who holds office and when you vote. Nothing more.
  • Private. Never stored and never logged.

Next.js with Census, congress-legislators, Geocodio, and OpenStates data, designed in Claude Design and built with Claude Code.
