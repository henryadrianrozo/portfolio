Overview

XUsCurrency is a clean currency converter and tracker for Android, for travelers and anyone sending money across borders. No ads and no clutter, just fast answers.

Highlights

  • Fast conversions. Convert between currencies with rates updated daily.
  • History charts. Charts show how the pairs you follow have moved.
  • Rate alerts. Get notified when a rate crosses a threshold you set.
  • Quick Settings tile. Open it from Quick Settings on any screen.
  • Accessible. Light and dark themes, with every control labeled and easy to tap.
  • Free, no ads. No ads, powered by the open-source fawazahmed0 currency API.

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, built with Android Studio, Claude Code, and ChatGPT, starting from no prior coding experience. Live on Google Play.
