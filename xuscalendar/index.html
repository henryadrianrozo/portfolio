Overview

XUsCalendar turns the event in front of you into a calendar entry in seconds. Paste a message, drop a screenshot of a flyer, or share an Instagram link, check the details, and add it.

Highlights

  • One box, three inputs. Text, an image, or an Instagram link. No mode to pick.
  • Reads flyers visually. It uses layout and emphasis, not just the words.
  • Never makes things up. If the address or end time is not there, it stays blank.
  • Quick review. Every assumption is spelled out before you save.
  • Straight to your calendar. One tap to Google Calendar, or an .ics file for any other app.
  • Nothing kept. No account, no dashboard, and nothing saved.
