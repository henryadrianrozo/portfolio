Overview

XUsNotes is a blank page that opens instantly, for the things you type that do not need an account: a number to hold, a paragraph to reword, text to strip of its formatting.

Highlights

  • Ready to type. Opens to a new note with the cursor waiting. Empty notes clean themselves up.
  • Rich text. Headings, lists, and checklists when you need them.
  • Stays tidy. Star and sort notes, or auto-delete after 30 days. Starred notes are kept.
  • Clean paste. Pasted text keeps the words and drops the clutter.
  • Private. Nothing uploaded, nothing synced, no account.
  • Simple on purpose. No tags, folders, or sharing to manage.

A single HTML file with no build step, designed in Claude Design and built with Claude Code.
