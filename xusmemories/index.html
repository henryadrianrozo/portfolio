Overview

XUsMemories is a video diary for the moments you want to keep. Open it, tap one red button, and talk. Memories are small, private, and easy to find again.

Highlights

  • One button to record. Front or back camera, with optional notes while you talk.
  • Small files, made to last. Years of memories fit comfortably on one phone.
  • Diaries and tags. Ongoing chapters like a move, a new job, or a pregnancy.
  • Find anything. Browse by list or calendar, or search titles, notes, and tags.
  • Watch it again later. Set a reminder to rewatch a memory years from now.
  • Always yours. Saved to your phone and your iCloud. No account, ads, or tracking.

Native SwiftUI with CloudKit sync, built with Claude Code. Live on the App Store.
